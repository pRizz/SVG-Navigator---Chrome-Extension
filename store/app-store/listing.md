### Description [4000]

<!-- description -->
SVG Navigator adds infinite zoom and panning to the SVG files you open in Safari. SVGs are vector graphics that stay sharp at any size, so you can keep zooming into big maps, diagrams, and illustrations for as long as there is detail to see.

Open an SVG file and:
• Scroll to zoom in infinitely, or back out, at the pointer
• Drag to pan, or hold Space and move the pointer
• Drag a box around exactly the area you want to see (zoom box mode)
• Press Esc to reset the view, or Option to zoom out
• Use the + / − / Reset toolbar in the corner of the page

Make it work your way from the toolbar button:
• Choose whether click-and-drag pans or draws a zoom box
• Adjust scroll sensitivity, or invert the scroll direction
• Show or hide the zoom toolbar, and let it fade out when you don't need it
• Pick a background color for transparent SVGs

SVG Navigator works on SVG files from any website, including ones served without a .svg file name. It leaves other pages alone.

Getting started: open the SVG Navigator app, turn on the extension in Safari Settings > Extensions, and allow it on every website so it can run on the SVG files you open.

Private by design: SVG Navigator collects no data and makes no network requests. Your settings stay in Safari.

SVG Navigator is free and open source, and is also available for Chrome and Firefox.
<!-- /description -->

SVG Navigator is a Safari web extension. To try it: open the app, click the button to open Safari Settings, turn on SVG Navigator under Extensions, then open https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/17/World.svg. When Safari asks, allow the extension on the site. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, and press Esc to reset. The toolbar button opens the settings.
